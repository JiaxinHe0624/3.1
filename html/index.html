About the App

Many students juggle multiple classes at the same time. They usually write down their assignments on paper or spreadsheets, but these get messy fast when several deadlines pile up in one week. Study Planner puts all your schoolwork in one simple list.

Each task has a title, the class it is for, a deadline and extra notes. The list sorts by due date, so your most urgent work shows up at the top. Everything saves locally in your browser — you wonot lose your task list even if you refresh the page.

Key features

  • Add a study task: fill in the task title, course code, course name, due date, description and notes.
  • Edit or remove tasks you have already made; you will get a pop-up check before anything gets deleted.
  • See all your tasks laid out in a table. Any work that is past due will stand out.
  • It checks your input: you can not leave boxes blank or pick a date that does not make sense.
  • Login required before the task page opens.
